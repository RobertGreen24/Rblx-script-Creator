How it works

1
Pick features. Sprint, coins, kill bricks, teams, a round loop - whatever your game needs.
2
Tune the numbers. Walk speed, coin value, autosave interval, admin UserIds. Every option has a sensible default.
3
Generate. You get a file tree, a combined preview, and a .zip with a Rojo project file.
4
Edit and save. Tweak anything in the built-in editor and store it in your project list.
